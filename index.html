I have a long-standing interest in robotics. My goal is to build a general pipeline or benchmark for translating robot learning algorithms into real-world systems, covering the full workflow from teleoperation and data collection to model training, deployment, and evaluation.

I am a PhD student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Renjing Xu. My current interests center on dexterous manipulation and vision-language-action models.

Before my PhD, I worked on robot software integration and algorithm infrastructure at the Hong Kong Centre for Logistics Robotics. I received my master's degree from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, where I worked with Prof. Yunhui Liu's group.

我长期专注于机器人领域的研究，希望搭建一套面向通用机器人算法落地的 pipeline 或 benchmark， 覆盖从遥操作与数据采集、模型训练到部署落地与评测的完整流程。

我目前在香港科技大学（广州）攻读博士（2026 年 9 月入学），导师是 许人镜教授。 目前主要研究灵巧操作与视觉—语言—动作模型（VLA）。

在攻读博士前，我曾在香港物流机器人研究中心从事机器人软件系统集成和算法基础设施建设。 硕士毕业于香港中文大学，曾在刘云辉教授团队开展研究。
